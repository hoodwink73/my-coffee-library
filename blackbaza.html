03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer: Ode 6 → 5.1. Then go hotter, up to 96 °C.

Boozy, too much ferment → drop to 90 °C first. If it stays boozy, try a hot-then-cool Switch hybrid.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser: Ode 6 → 6.2, and pour more gently.

picked for
a flat bed, hotter and coarser

Orea's two-cup recipe for fermented coffees: coarse, hot, five even pulses on a flat bed. It runs longer and hotter than the cone, so the orange should come out cleaner. Orea says to grind a touch finer on Sibarist paper, hence Ode 7.1 rather than 8.

why this recipe
picked for
a two-temperature valve routine

Fukahori won the 2018 World Brewers Cup with this: a cool closed steep for sweetness, a hot open pour for acidity, then a cool closed finish for juicy body. Written for a fermented coffee, it should lift the mandarin over the cocoa. Brewed on a GINA valve dripper; the Switch does the same job.
