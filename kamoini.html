03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, hollow → finer one move, Ode 5.1 → 4.2, then hotter: 88 → 92 → 96 °C.

Bitter, drying → coarser, Ode 6, or pour more gently.

Harsh but not drying → lengthen the ratio to 1:17, 20 g : 340 g.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Coffee ad Astra

Spin length

Brew it twice: a 2 s swirl, then a 7 s swirl after the last pour. Time both drawdowns.

expect · Heavy spinning sends fines down and can clog the paper.

try it on · Kamunyaka AA guide

EXP 02B · Philocoffea

Sweet vs bright

Keep →200, →260, →320. Split the first 140 g as 40 + 100, then 90 + 50.

expect · A smaller first pour is sweeter; 90 + 50 is the one for yuzu.

try it on · Kamunyaka AA guide

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Acidity and body read best warm; sweetness rises as it cools.

try it on · any of the three
