03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer — ZP6 3.9 → 3.4; still sour, brew at 98 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser — ZP6 3.9 → 4.4, and pour more gently.

Sour and bitter at once → fix evenness first: lower, gentler pours and a swirl after the bloom.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Coffee ad Astra

Spin length

After the last pour, give a 2 s swirl one day, 7 s the next. Time the drawdown.

expect · Heavy spinning drives fines down and can slow or clog the paper.

try it on · The roaster's own numbers

EXP 02B · Philocoffea

Sweet vs bright

Make the first two pours 40 + 60 g, then next time 60 + 40 g.

expect · Smaller first pour = sweeter. Larger = brighter.

try it on · The roaster's own numbers

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Red fruit hot, acidity warm, cane sugar as it cools.
