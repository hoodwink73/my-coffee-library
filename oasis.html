03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer, Ode 5.0 → 4.1 on the O1, then up from 93 °C in 2 °C steps.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser, Ode 5.0 → 5.2, or pour more gently.

Sour and bitter at once → fix evenness before grind: gentler pours, or a 13 g dose.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Orea, Cafec

Paper swap

Brew the Onyx recipe on Sibarist FAST Flat O1, then on Orea Wave O1 at the same grind.

expect · The flat paper reads juicier and fuller, the wave one lighter.

try it on · La Margarita Gesha guide

EXP 02B · Philocoffea

Sweet vs bright split

Keep 30 g bloom and 170 g total. Pour to 100 g, then try 70 g.

expect · A smaller first pour is sweeter, a larger one brighter.

try it on · La Margarita Gesha guide

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Citrus hot, jasmine warm, sweetness as it cools.

try it on · any of the three
