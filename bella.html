03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Still sour or hollow → one more step finer, Ode 7 → 6.2, then up to 100 °C.

Bitter or drying → back half a step, Ode 7.1.

Boozy or harsh → kettle down to 95 °C, then try the Switch hybrid.
