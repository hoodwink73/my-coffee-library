03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, done before 2:40 → grind finer, Ode 4.1 → 3.2. Your earlier cup at Ode 7.2 was thin too.

Bitter, drying, or stalls past 3:15 → grind coarser, Ode 4.1 → 5.0, and soften the heavy spiral.

Boozy → about 3 °C cooler, 97 °C. Still boozy: the Kasuya hot-then-cool Switch hybrid.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Coffee ad Astra

Spin length

After the last pour, swirl 2 s. Next brew, 7 s. Time both drawdowns.

expect · Heavy spinning sends fines down and can clog the paper.

try it on · El Vergel Co-Ferment guide

EXP 02A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength moves the cup most; find the tropical sweet spot.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · The roaster says stone fruit and florals hot, pineapple and papaya as it cools.
