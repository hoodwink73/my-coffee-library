03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → steep 30 s longer before you press.

Bitter, drying, hollow → one step coarser, Ode 5.1 → 6, and stir less.

Sour and bitter at once → fix the pour first: hold the timings, a smaller dose.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split the AeroPress cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength had the greatest impact on the cup in the UC Davis work.

try it on · Cold bloom

EXP 02B · Philocoffea

Sweet vs bright

Make the first pour 40 g, then 60 g. Keep the rest fixed.

expect · A smaller first pour reads sweeter, a larger one brighter.

try it on · Five equal pours

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Your V60 cup already got sweeter and more floral as it cooled.
