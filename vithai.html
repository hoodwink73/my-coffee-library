03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser, Ode 5.1 → 6.0, and pour more gently.

Sour, thin → grind finer, Ode 5.1 → 4.2. Still sour: kettle up to 92 °C, Blue Tokai's own.

Boozy → about 3 °C cooler, 85 °C. Still boozy: the Kasuya hot-then-cool Switch hybrid.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Coffee ad Astra

Spin length

After the last pour, swirl 2 s. Next brew, 7 s. Time both drawdowns.

expect · Heavy spinning sends fines down and can clog the paper.

try it on · Kerehaklu Blossom Culture pour-over

EXP 02A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength moves the cup most; find where jaggery turns to lime.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Acidity and body read best warm; sweetness rises as it cools.

try it on · any of the three

wildcards, if you're feeling it · Melodrip vs kettle · Water swap · Flash-iced · Grinder swap · Days off roast · Device swap: V60 ↔ Z1
