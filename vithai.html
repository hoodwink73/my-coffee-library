03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, hollow → grind finer, Ode 7.1 → 6.2. Still sour: kettle up 2 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser, Ode 7.1 → 8.

Boozy → about 3 °C cooler, 89 °C, then try the Switch hybrid.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01A · SCA, Batali 2020

Temperature pair

Brew the Aussie at 92 °C, then at 86 °C, same grind.

expect · The hotter cup extracts more. At matched extraction, 87–93 °C taste alike.

try it on · The Aussie

EXP 02A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength changes the cup more than anything else.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Acidity and body read best warm; sweetness rises as it cools.

try it on · any of the three
