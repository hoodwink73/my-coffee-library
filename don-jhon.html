03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer — Ode 3.2 → 3.0, same pours. Still sour: go to 98 °C.

Boozy, hot-liquor edge → drop to 93 °C, same grind (C). Still boozy: brew the Switch hybrid.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser — Ode 3.2 → 4.1, and pour more gently.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Coffee ad Astra

Spin length

After the last pour, swirl 2 s one brew and 7 s the next. Time the drawdown.

expect · Heavy spinning drives fines down and can clog the paper: slower drawdown, drier cup.

try it on · La Papaya extraction guide

EXP 02A · Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways; add 0, 15 and 30 g hot water.

expect · Strength moves the taste more than anything else. Find where the candy shows.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Fruit hot, acidity warm, strawberry-candy sweetness as it cools.

try it on · any of the three
