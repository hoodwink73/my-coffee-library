03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → go 2 clicks finer, Ode 6.2 → 6, water at its hottest.

Bitter, drying → go coarser and pour gentler; naturals clog, so swirl less.

Thin and flat after a fix → possibly stale beans: the label reads 27/11/2025.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Orea

Paper swap

On the Stagg [X], keep the recipe and grind. Change only the paper.

expect · Flat beds read juicy, sweet and full; wave papers lighter.

try it on · Deri Kidame natural

EXP 02A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength changes the cup more than anything else.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Acidity and body read best warm; sweetness rises as it cools.

try it on · any of the three
