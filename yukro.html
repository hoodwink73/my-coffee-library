03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin → finer one move, Ode 7.2 → 7, then hotter, up to 100 °C.

Bitter, drying → coarser one move, Ode 8.1, or stir more gently.

Harsh but not drying → too strong: add more dilution water first.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split the finished AeroPress cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Shows how much dilution this old coffee wants.

try it on · Champion AeroPress

EXP 02B · Philocoffea

Sweet vs bright

Keep →250 and →350. Split the first 150 g as 50 + 100, then 80 + 70.

expect · A smaller first pour is sweeter; 80 + 70 is the one for lemon.

try it on · Stagg [X] flat bed

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Acidity and body read best warm; sweetness rises as it cools.

try it on · any of the three
