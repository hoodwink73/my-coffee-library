03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer: Ode 6.2 → 6.0 (C40 24). Still sour: go to 94, then 96 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser: Ode 6.2 → 7.1 (C40 28), and make the closed-valve pour gentler.

Boozy, overripe → drop to 89 °C first. Still boozy: finish the steep with cooler water (Kasuya hot-then-cool).

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Rao; Coffee ad Astra

Switch valve

Brew the hybrid again with the valve open the whole time. Same grind, same water.

expect · Closed is heavier and rounder; open is cleaner and lighter.

try it on · Competition Switch hybrid

EXP 02A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength moves the cup more than anything else. Find where chocolate turns to vanilla.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping; Driftaway

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Driftaway's arc: chocolate-orange hot, vanilla cream warm, bubblegum cool.

try it on · any of the three

Liftee's 2021 World Brewers Cup routine (3rd place) for a fermented Colombian: small early pours, larger late ones, about three quarters of the water through the Melodrip.
