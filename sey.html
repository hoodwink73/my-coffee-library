03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer, Ode 5.0 → 4.2. Still sour: kettle to 98, then 100 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser, Ode 5.0 → 5.2, and soften the heavy spiral.

Sour and bitter, or stalls past 4:00 → fix evenness first: gentle spiral instead of heavy, bloom through the Melodrip, or a 13 g dose.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Orea

Paper swap

Brew the Onyx recipe on Orea Wave O1, then on Sibarist FAST Flat O1. Same grind; time both.

expect · Orea: flat paper is juicier and fuller, wave lighter. FAST paper drains quicker.

try it on · Peru La Margarita Gesha guide

EXP 02B · Philocoffea

Sweet vs bright

Keep →170 and →240. Split the first 100 g as 30 + 70, then 60 + 40.

expect · A smaller first share reads sweeter (blueberry), a larger one brighter (mandarin).

try it on · Peru La Margarita Gesha guide

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Acidity and body read best warm; sweetness rises as it cools.

try it on · any of the three

wildcards, if you're feeling it · Melodrip vs kettle · Water swap · Flash-iced · Grinder swap · Days off roast · Device swap: O1 ↔ Stagg [X]

Hedrick's micro brew for light washed Geshas: a tiny dose in the narrow Deep 27 makes a deep bed, and many gentle pours stop water slipping past it. He gives no temperature, so the kettle is at 98 °C for a very light roast.
