03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour or thin → grind finer, Ode 5.2 → 5, then 100 °C.

Bitter or drying → grind coarser, Ode 5.2 → 6.1.

Sour and bitter, or stalls → gentler pours, Melodrip first pour, a 13 g dose.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01A · SCA, Batali 2020

Temperature pair

Brew the Dara at 98 °C and at 92 °C, same grind.

expect · The hotter cup extracts more, so they'll differ.

try it on · The Dara, run hot

EXP 02B · Philocoffea

Sweet vs bright

Keep the last 150 g. Split the first 100 g as 40 + 60, then 60 + 40.

expect · A smaller first pour is sweeter, a larger one brighter: blueberry against mandarin.

try it on · The Dara, run hot

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Acidity and body read best warm; sweetness rises as it cools.

try it on · any of the three

wildcards, if you're feeling it · Melodrip vs kettle · Water swap · Flash-iced · Grinder swap · Days off roast
