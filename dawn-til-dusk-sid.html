03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer — ZP6 3.9 → 3.4, keep 90 °C; still sour, go to 93 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser — ZP6 3.9 → 4.4, and pour more gently.

Harsh but not drying → too strong: same grind, go 15 : 240 g.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Coffee ad Astra

Spin length

After the last pour, give a 2 s swirl one day, 7 s the next. Time the drawdown.

expect · Heavy spinning drives fines down and can slow or clog the paper.

try it on · Three equal pours

EXP 02A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways; add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength changes the cup most — find where chocolate and grapefruit balance.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Chocolate hot, grapefruit warm, sweetness as it cools.

try it on · any of the three

why this recipe
picked for
a different brewer, a single small cup

The narrow Deep 27 builds a deep bed even from 8 g; many small laminar pours stop water bypassing, and a coarse grind keeps it from turning bitter. A lighter, cleaner cup that lets the grapefruit show.

why this recipe
picked for
a different style: steep, pour, steep

Cool immersion for sweetness, a hot pour for acidity, cool immersion again for juicy body. On a chocolate-forward coffee it should give the sweetest, roundest cup of the three.
