03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer, Ode 5.2 → 5.0. Hotter, up to 94 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser, Ode 5.2 → 6.1, and pour more gently.

Sour and bitter at once → fix evenness before grind: gentler pours or the Melodrip, and agitate the bloom.
