03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Boozy, hot-sugar → drop to 89 °C first (−3 °C), same grind. Still boozy: try the Aussie on the Z1.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer — Ode 5.2 → 5.0. You can also go hotter, up to 96 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser — Ode 5.2 → 6.1, and pour the first 100 g more gently.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Rao; Coffee ad Astra

Valve open vs closed

Brew it twice: as written, then with the valve open the whole time. Same grind, same 2:10.

expect · The steep is heavier and rounder, with less risk of astringency.

try it on · Percolate, then steep

EXP 02A · UC Davis 2020; Yettu

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways; add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Yettu says the cola mouthfeel shows when it's brewed stronger. Find where cola gives way to mosambi.

try it on · The Aussie

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Flavour hot, acidity warm, sweetness as it cools.

try it on · Percolate, then steep
