03 · if the first cup is…

One change per brew.
Numbers are for the suggested recipe.

Sour, thin, short → grind finer on the V60, Ode 5.1 → 4.2; you can also go hotter, up to 96 °C.

Bitter, drying → grind coarser, Ode 5.1 → 6.0, and pour lower and gentler.

Boozy → drop the water about 3 °C, to 87 °C. Still boozy: try the Switch.

04 · three experiments, one change each

Tear one off.

EXP 01B · Coffee ad Astra

Spin length

Finish with a 2 s swirl one brew, a 7 s swirl the next. Time both drawdowns.

expect · Heavy spinning drives fines down: slower drawdown, heavier cup.

EXP 02A · UC Davis, Batali 2020

Strength ladder

Split one cup three ways. Add 0, 15 and 30 g of hot water.

expect · Strength changes the cup more than anything else. Find where the violet shows.

try it on · any of the three

EXP 03B · SCA cupping

Taste it cooling

Sip at about 70, 60 and 45 °C, then at room temperature.

expect · Flavour hot, acidity warm, sweetness and watermelon as it cools.

why this recipe
matched on
light roast · heavy-ferment process · Ethiopian-line rare variety

ONA wrote this for an anaerobic Sidra, the closest cousin in the library. Five equal pulses at a moderate 90 °C keep the ferment from turning boozy. The start times are when the bed usually drains.
